Client-side sync encryption

If you create an account, your private account snapshot is encrypted on your device using AES-256-GCM before cloud sync. Grades, assignments, schedules and the default private course list are not stored as readable server-side fields. Enabling course sharing explicitly uploads limited course identifiers and catalog titles for classrooms; it is separate from opting into peer discovery. Selected school, flashcard identifiers for moderation, shared content, game records and daily study-time totals are stored separately for their features. Classroom tools do not collect private grade or assignment summaries or provide teacher progress rosters. Public study statistics require a separate opt-in.

Local browser storage is not encrypted by this mechanism. Supabase receives your password for authentication, so this is not a strict zero-knowledge system. Read the Privacy Policy for data categories and sharing limits.

You may export an independently passphrase-encrypted study backup or permanently delete your account from Data settings. Keep backup passphrases safe; they cannot be recovered. Account deletion removes active account-linked records and authored shared content, not independent copies, downloaded backups or provider-retained backups/logs. Use the private data-request form, not public issues, for account-specific concerns.
