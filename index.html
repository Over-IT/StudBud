                      About StudBud

                      Purpose

                      StudBud is a study companion built for students. It keeps your grades, GPA, assignments, schedule and flashcards in one place, and makes studying more rewarding with leaderboards, an arcade, and multiplayer games that run on your own flashcards.

                      How to use it

                      • Dashboard: see upcoming quizzes, study suggestions and your progress at a glance. Add a quiz or test to get a study plan.
                      • GPA & What-If: enter classes and assignments (decimals like 23.21/25 work), then try What-If scenarios by editing or adding grades.
                      • Planner, Calendar & Schedule: track assignments, due dates and your school day.
                      • Flashcards & Import Cards: create cards by hand or import them, then flip and study them as many times as you like.
                      • Practice & Arcade / Multiplayer: answer questions from your cards to power games, alone or with other students.
                      • Leaderboards & Shop: compete on study time and spend coins on avatar items.
                      • Settings: change your username (once a week), theme, sounds, and replay the tutorial.
