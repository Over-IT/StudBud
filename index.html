                About StudBud

                Purpose

                StudBud is a study companion built for students. It keeps your grades, GPA, assignments, schedule and flashcards in one place, and makes studying more rewarding with leaderboards, an arcade, and multiplayer games that run on your own flashcards.

                How to use it

                • Home: see upcoming work, your automatic study clock and progress at a glance. Add a quiz or test to get a study plan.
                • My classes: enter classes and grades (decimals like 23.21/25 work). What-if grades lets you try possible scores without changing your actual grades.
                • Import from Infinite Campus: open My classes to bring in grades, assignments, categories, class times and the school calendar. The import dialog includes computer and phone instructions.
                • Planner: use Day, Week or Month to see deadlines, work dates and personal events. Day suggests what to work on first. Tasks is a separate page for managing assignments, completion and grades.
                • Study: Practice is for studying, Manage sets is for creating and editing, and Discover is for community materials. Focus practice prioritizes terms needing recall using recent answers. Tests & drills offers more ways to practice.
                • Singleplayer: play the same game modes on your own with computer players. Host or join a multiplayer room to play with other students. Flashcard quizzes and practice tests remain available separately.
                • Leaderboards & Shop: compete on study time and spend coins on avatar items.
                • Settings: choose a start page and study defaults, edit school/GPA details, adjust appearance or audio, export backups and replay the tutorial. Change your username and privacy options in Profile.
