                      About StudBud

                      Purpose

                      StudBud is a study companion built for students. It keeps your grades, GPA, assignments, schedule and flashcards in one place, and makes studying more rewarding with leaderboards, an arcade, and multiplayer games that run on your own flashcards.

                      How to use it

                      • Dashboard: see upcoming quizzes, study suggestions and your progress at a glance. Add a quiz or test to get a study plan.
                      • GPA & Hypothetical Grades: enter classes and assignments (decimals like 23.21/25 work), then try possible scores without changing your actual grades.
                      • Import from Infinite Campus: on the GPA page, bring in your classes, grades, assignments, categories, class times and school calendar in a few clicks.
                      • Planner, Calendar & Schedule: track assignments, due dates and your school day.
                      • Flashcards & Import Cards: create or import sets and study anytime. Learning progress helps prioritize practice; flashcards are not assignments and have no deadlines.
                      • Practice: quiz yourself with flashcards or try multiplayer game modes solo with simulated players. Open Multiplayer to play with other students.
                      • Leaderboards & Shop: compete on study time and spend coins on avatar items.
                      • Settings: change your username (once a week), theme, sounds, and replay the tutorial.

                        Import from Infinite Campus

                        Imports classes, grades, assignments and schedule. StudBud never sees your password.

                        1. Get the StudBud Import button (one time only).
                          StudBud Import Drag this up to your bookmarks bar
                          Don't see a bookmarks bar? Press Ctrl+Shift+B (⌘+Shift+B on Mac). Already added it? Skip to step 2.
                          On a phone or tablet? Set up the import bookmark Use Safari or Chrome in a browser tab, not the Campus app or an in-app browser. Bookmark any page, name it StudBud Import, then edit that bookmark and replace its entire address with the URL below. Keep the javascript: at the beginning.
                          Safari: use Share → Add Bookmark, then Bookmarks → Edit. Chrome for Android: bookmark a page using the star in the menu, then open Bookmarks and edit its address. Once you are signed in on the Campus website, run the saved bookmark. In Safari, choose it from Bookmarks. In Chrome for Android, type StudBud Import in the address bar and tap the bookmark suggestion, rather than a search result. Do not paste the URL into a search box.
                        2. Open Infinite Campus and sign in.
                          Different Campus address? Paste the address you use for the Campus Student portal.
                        3. On the Campus tab, run your saved StudBud Import bookmark. It gathers your data and sends it back here. Come back to this tab to check your classes and finish.
                          Can't use bookmarks? Use the console instead
                          On the Campus tab press F12, open Console, paste the script and press Enter. If Chrome asks, type allow pasting first.
                        4. Nothing showed up here? On a phone, tabs may not send data directly. In the Campus helper, tap Copy data, then return here and paste it. If copying is blocked, use Show data to select and copy it manually, or download the .json file.
