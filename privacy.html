Privacy Policy

Last updated October 17, 2026

What StudBud stores

Classes, grades, assignments, flashcards, study history and settings are saved in your browser on your own device. Guest study data stays on your device unless you choose an online feature, such as joining a live game. Loading the website also contacts our hosting and library providers, and enabled advertising contacts Google as described below.

If you create an account, your username, school, coins, purchases, public decks and synced study data are stored with our database provider (Supabase) so they follow you between devices. Accounts use a username and password; the app does not collect email addresses or send email verification or sign-in codes. Removing these features does not automatically delete any email previously linked to an existing Supabase account; contact the site administrator to manage previously stored account information. Passwords are handled by Supabase authentication and are never stored in plain text. Your school and real name are only shown publicly if you turn that on in your profile.

Infinite Campus imports run in your browser from a file or page you provide; StudBud never receives your Infinite Campus password.

Advertising

StudBud may show banner ads from Google AdSense on a few browsing pages (home, decks and the shop). Ads are never shown during studying, tutorials, games, or pop-ups, and are never required to use any feature.

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on visits to this and other sites. You can opt out of personalized advertising in Google Ad Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors' cookies at aboutads.info.

Learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Children

StudBud is built for high school students and is intended for users 13 and older.

Your choices

You can erase data from this device at any time from Settings → Profile & Data. Account data syncs automatically while connected. To request a copy of your account data or have an account and its synced data deleted from our database, use the contact link below.
