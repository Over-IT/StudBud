Privacy Policy

Last updated October 20, 2026

What StudBud stores

Classes, grades, assignments, flashcards, study history and settings are saved in your browser on your own device. Guest study data stays on your device unless you choose an online feature, such as joining a live game. Loading the website also contacts our hosting and library providers, and enabled advertising contacts Google as described below.

If you create an account, your username, school, coins, purchases, public decks and synced study data are stored with our database provider (Supabase) so they follow you between devices. Accounts use a username and password; the app does not collect email addresses or send email verification or sign-in codes. Removing these features does not automatically delete any email previously linked to an existing Supabase account; contact the site administrator to manage previously stored account information. Passwords are handled by Supabase authentication and are never stored in plain text. Your school and real name are only shown publicly if you turn that on in your profile.

Infinite Campus imports run in your browser from a file or page you provide; StudBud never receives your Infinite Campus password.

Automatic study tracking stores active-time estimates and recent answer results to help you plan and find terms needing practice. It pauses when StudBud is hidden or unfocused, after 60 seconds without interaction, and outside question-answering portions of games. It does not monitor other apps, record your screen, or verify that you are paying attention.

Optional shared-course discovery

Course discovery is off by default. If you enable “Let students sharing a course find my username” in Profile, other opted-in students at the same supported school can find your username when you have a course in common for their selected semester. They can open your public profile, which follows your other visibility settings. Enabling discovery reveals that shared course even if your public school or course list is otherwise hidden.

Discovery never shares your grades, assignments, study history or real name. Matching uses self-reported, synced courses, not verified teacher or period rosters. You can turn discovery off again in Profile to stop appearing in future searches.

School community and class discussions

Schoolmate discovery is a separate choice, off by default, in School → Community. Turning it on makes your username and school discoverable to other opted-in members of your school, even if your public school badge is hidden. You can turn it off there at any time. It does not reveal your grades, real name, assignments or schedule. Community set lists include only sets their owners intentionally published. School-scoped lists require the author to show a public school badge or both viewer and author to enable school discovery; publishing alone does not reveal a hidden school.

Class announcements, discussion posts and replies are visible to the class teacher and current members of that class. They are associated with your username and stored with Supabase. Classes use self-reported course membership, not verified teacher or period rosters; do not post private or sensitive information. Authors can remove their own posts and teachers can remove class posts. Removed content is hidden from class views; moderation records and reports may retain content for investigation. Reporting sends your explanation and the referenced class/post context privately to staff.

Teachers can list their live games on a class page. Joining uses the same room codes and game rules as other multiplayer rooms; a class listing is not a private classroom access restriction.

Advertising

StudBud may show banner ads from Google AdSense on a few browsing pages (home, decks and the shop). Ads are never shown during studying, tutorials, games, or pop-ups, and are never required to use any feature.

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on visits to this and other sites. You can opt out of personalized advertising in Google Ad Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors' cookies at aboutads.info.

Learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Children

StudBud is built for high school students and is intended for users 13 and older.

Your choices

You can erase data from this device at any time from Settings → Profile & Data. Account data syncs automatically while connected. To request a copy of your account data or have an account and its synced data deleted from our database, use the contact link below.
