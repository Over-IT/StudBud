Privacy Policy

Last updated November 13, 2026

What StudBud stores

Classes, grades, assignments, flashcards, study history and settings are saved in your browser on your own device. Guest study data stays on your device unless you choose an online feature, such as joining a live game. Loading the website also contacts our hosting and library providers, and enabled advertising contacts Google as described below.

The local browser copy is not encrypted by StudBud's cloud-sync encryption. Anyone with access to your unlocked browser profile may be able to access it. Use a private device or separate browser profile, and sign out when finished on a shared device.

If you finish a school year, a private read-only archive keeps that year's classes, final grades and credits, assignments, calendar, grade scenarios and schedule. Final transcript grades contribute to cumulative GPA even when you reset current classes. Archives follow the same device storage, account sync and backup rules as your other study data; finishing a year does not publish them.

When you sign in, StudBud encrypts your private synced account snapshot in your browser with AES-256-GCM. The encryption key is derived from your account password using PBKDF2-SHA-256 before the snapshot is uploaded; the snapshot is not stored in plaintext. Supabase receives your password for authentication, so this is not a strict zero-knowledge guarantee. You must enter your account password on a device that needs to unlock encrypted data. StudBud cannot recover encrypted data if you lose or reset the password, and changing the password does not re-encrypt an existing snapshot. Keep your password safe.

Your course list stays inside your encrypted account snapshot by default. If you enable “Enable course sharing for classrooms” in Profile, a readable index of course codes/terms and public catalog titles is uploaded for classroom matching and optional course discovery. Custom class titles stay encrypted; unsupported schools upload no readable course list. Course sharing does not upload grades, assignments, schedules, flashcard contents or study history as readable fields. Turning it off removes the readable course index and classroom memberships from the active database; server checks prevent older clients from restoring that index while sharing is off. Previously shared content and provider backups are not recalled.

Your selected school and flashcard-set identifiers used for moderation remain readable to the service. Explicitly shared sets, username/profile preferences, multiplayer purchases, selected game records, daily study-time totals and community/classroom content are stored separately to provide those features. The whole service is not zero-knowledge, and usernames or school/course associations can still indirectly identify someone.

Public profiles use your username and can show an optional favorite subject and activity statistics. They do not display a real name, school tag, grade/year, bio or free-text study goal. Course and school discovery are separate opt-ins and can reveal your username and school/course association to other participating students. Choose a username that does not identify you, and do not post names, contact details, student IDs, or other sensitive information in public or community content.

Accounts use a username and password; the app does not collect email addresses or send email verification or sign-in codes. Passwords are handled by Supabase authentication and are never stored in plain text. You can manually enter classes and assignments or paste and review supported Infinite Campus data; previously saved imported schedules remain on your device/account unless you remove them.

Automatic study tracking stores active-time estimates and recent answer results to help you plan and find terms needing practice. It pauses when StudBud is hidden or unfocused, after 60 seconds without interaction, and outside question-answering portions of games. It does not monitor other apps, record your screen, or verify that you are paying attention.

Private session details remain in your encrypted account snapshot. Daily study-time totals are stored separately for personal goals and streaks. Sharing time and streaks on public profiles, with buds and on leaderboards is off by default and requires enabling it in Profile. The privacy update requires fresh consent from existing accounts too. Turning sharing off hides those statistics from future profile and leaderboard requests; it does not delete the underlying totals or copies others already saved. Game results and intentionally shared content remain visible according to their feature's audience.

Optional shared-course discovery

Course discovery is off by default and requires enabling course sharing first. If you also enable “Let students sharing a course find my username” in Profile, other opted-in students at the same supported school can find your username when you have a course in common for their selected semester. They can open your pseudonymous profile. Enabling discovery reveals that shared course even if your profile does not show a school tag.

Discovery does not share your grades, assignments, schedule, study history or real name. Matching uses self-reported courses, not verified teacher or period rosters. You can turn discovery off again in Profile to stop appearing in future searches.

School community and class discussions

Schoolmate discovery is a separate choice, off by default, in School → Community. Turning it on makes your username and school discoverable to other opted-in members of your school, even if your profile does not show a school tag. You can turn it off there at any time. It does not reveal your grades, real name, assignments or schedule. Community set lists include only sets their owners intentionally published. School-scoped lists require the author to enable school discovery; publishing alone does not reveal a hidden school.

Course communities are available without a teacher after enabling course sharing, based on your synced course codes and supported school. Class announcements, discussion posts and replies are visible to current members and authorized moderators. They are associated with your username and stored with Supabase. Classes use self-reported course membership, not verified teacher or period rosters; do not post private or sensitive information. Authors can remove their own posts, matching course teachers can remove class posts, and authorized moderators can review and remove inappropriate shared content. Removed content is hidden from class views; moderation records and reports may retain content for investigation. Reporting sends your explanation and the referenced class/post or shared-set context privately to staff.

Sharing a set with a class defaults to course-only (the same course at the same school). You can explicitly choose school-only or public; public sharing publishes its title, terms, answers, distractors, course and purpose note to the public library. Class/school sharing identifies the course/school association to the chosen audience even if profile discovery is off. Study history, private assessment links, images and attachments are not copied. Existing public copies must be unpublished separately before sharing a set privately. People can save independent personal copies; removing a share does not recall previously saved copies.

Teachers can publish sets, announcements and live games, but classroom tools do not collect or expose students' private grades, missing work, upcoming assignments, study sessions or activity timestamps. There is no teacher progress roster or roster export. Moderators can review shared community content within their permitted schools; administrators can review communities across supported schools. Those permissions do not unlock encrypted account snapshots. Study statistics you explicitly make public can still be viewed by other signed-in users, including teachers.

Teachers can list their live games on a class page. Joining uses the same room codes and game rules as other multiplayer rooms; a class listing is not a private classroom access restriction.

Advertising

StudBud may show banner ads from Google AdSense on a few browsing pages (home, decks and the shop). Ads are never shown during studying, tutorials, games, or pop-ups, and are never required to use any feature.

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on visits to this and other sites. You can opt out of personalized advertising in Google Ad Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors' cookies at aboutads.info.

Your choices

Settings → Profile & Data lets you export or restore an encrypted study-data backup, including when using local/guest mode. Choose a separate passphrase of at least 12 characters and keep it safe; we cannot recover it. The download has no readable school/course index. Restoring replaces local study data and syncs it to the currently signed-in account; course-sharing consent is not transferred from the backup. Downloaded files are not removed when you sign out or delete your account.

You can clear this device's study data and sync baseline there without deleting your cloud account; synced data may download again. Signing out clears local study storage and reloads the page to clear in-memory views. Neither action removes browser history or backups you downloaded.

Permanent account deletion is also available there while signed in and online. It requires your username, a fresh password verification and any existing authenticator. It deletes your account and its active database records, including published sets, hosted games and authored class content, and detaches your player from other game rooms. Unsynced changes on this device are discarded. Clear offline copies on your other devices separately; they cannot be remotely erased. Copies other people saved independently are not recalled. Hosting/Auth logs and provider backups may remain under their providers' retention policies; active-database deletion does not guarantee their immediate erasure.

Use “Private account / data request” in Data settings for other access, correction or deletion concerns. Requests require a permanent account and are readable only by administrators, not ordinary moderators or other users. They are not encrypted private messaging and the form does not support private replies. Include only the minimum explanation, never passwords or student records. General bug/player reports remain available to authorized staff. Account data otherwise remains until you delete it or the operator removes it; no automatic deletion of inactive permanent accounts is promised.

Contact

Use the private in-app data-request form for account-specific concerns. General questions about this policy can be opened as an issue on GitHub. Issues are public: do not include passwords, student records, names or other sensitive information. If the private form is unavailable, do not move its contents to a public issue.
