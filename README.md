StudBud is a student hub with three parts: **Plan** (classes, assignments, grades, calendar), **Study** (flashcards, focus sessions, study streaks and leaderboards) and **Play** (live multiplayer games and arcade modes powered by your own flashcards, with a coin shop for characters, hats and accessories). Flashcards are study material, not assignments: every non-suspended card is available whenever you want, without due counts or daily study limits. Anki's SM-2 learning/relearning steps, Again/Hard/Good/Easy interval adjustments and leech detection still prioritize practice. Cram does not change learning progress. Only linked tests and quizzes have deadlines. Quiz and game questions are randomized independently.

## Open StudBud

After the GitHub Pages deployment finishes, open **https://mystudbud.org/** in Chrome, Safari, Edge, or Firefox. The site works on phones, tablets, Chromebooks, and computers without installing anything.

On Chrome for desktop, use the install icon in the address bar or choose **Install StudBud** from the browser menu. On Android, open the site in Chrome and choose **Add to Home screen**. On iPhone or iPad, open it in Safari and choose **Share → Add to Home Screen**.

The app shell is cached for offline loading after the first visit. Internet access is needed for account sign-in and syncing, externally hosted fonts and icons, and chart libraries.

## Ads

Optional, non-sticky banner slots sit at the bottom of Home, the decks list, and the shop. They stay collapsed until configured and are hidden offline, during tutorials, study sessions, games, and dialogs. There are no interstitials, rewarded ads, videos, or automatic refreshes.

Use manual horizontal units and keep AdSense Auto ads, anchor ads and vignette ads disabled in the AdSense account. Account-level automatic formats would bypass these footer-only placement rules.

In `ads.js`, fill in `window.STUDBUD_ADS.client` with your AdSense publisher ID (`ca-pub-XXXXXXXXXXXXXXXX`) and `slots.home`, `slots.decks`, and/or `slots.shop` with the corresponding ad-unit slot IDs. Leave any unused slot empty. The Google script loads only when a configured banner enters the viewport. Use `window.StudBudAds.refresh()` after changing configuration at runtime. Ad blockers or network errors leave the app usable.

Replace the commented example in `ads.txt` with your AdSense-authorized seller line and publish it at the **domain root** (for project-path hosting, this may require the root Pages repository or a custom domain). Follow AdSense approval, privacy/consent, and child-directed-content requirements before enabling ads; this integration does not supply a consent-management platform.

Team Tower Defense is a separate arcade mode; the existing study Tower Defense remains available. Two players form a 1v1, while larger rosters alternate between blue and red teams. Each side starts with 100 base HP and a shared $180 wallet. Walk anywhere with WASD or the joystick; choose an Archer, Cannon, Frost, Ballista, Tesla or Venom tower and click/tap an empty grid cell on your own side to build. Placement snaps to 70-unit cells, with a valid/invalid cell highlight, tower ghost and full range preview. Nothing is selected initially; placing or cancelling clears the preview, and every new tower requires another shop selection. Each team has a **40-tower maximum**, divided into equal rounded-down allowances among active teammates: `floor(40 / teammates)`, including yourself. Three teammates get 13 towers each. Late joins preserve existing towers but prevent further building above the new allowance or team cap. The shop has separate Build and Send tabs; selecting a friendly tower opens a separate management panel with ownership, stats and level-3 upgrades. Teammates can upgrade one another's towers using the shared wallet without changing ownership. Only owners can sell their towers, refunding **75% of build and upgrade costs**, rounded down, to the shared wallet; free staff-test towers refund nothing. The Send catalog includes Scouts, Runners, Brutes, Swarms, armored Sentinels and Wisps. Ballista bolts ignore armor, Tesla chains hit three targets, and Venom applies damage over time. Enemies enter the opposing team's marked path. Correct flashcard answers earn three times the room's configured answer reward for the shared wallet; defeated enemies pay the defending team a bounty. Automatic enemy waves begin after a 25-second grace period, then return 32 seconds after each wave is deployed. Each wave adds one enemy per side, with health rising 14% per wave (capped at 3.5×), speed rising 2.5% per wave (capped at 1.5×), and tougher enemy types joining later. Waves continue regardless of player orders; the first base to reach zero HP loses and results record wave progress. Idle teams typically fall within 2–3 minutes, while coordinated defense should last roughly 8–15 minutes. Chess gameplay is unchanged.

## Import from Infinite Campus

Use **Classes → Import from Infinite Campus** (also in **Settings → Data Management**) to bring in this school year's classes, grade categories and weights, all graded and upcoming assignments, class periods and times, and the school calendar.

1. On a computer, drag the **StudBud Import** button to your bookmarks bar (one time only; press Ctrl+Shift+B if the bar is hidden). On a phone/tablet, open the phone instructions, choose **Copy bookmark URL**, bookmark any page as **StudBud Import**, then edit that bookmark and replace its entire address with the copied URL, including `javascript:`.
2. Choose **Open Infinite Campus** and sign in. PCHS students get the Parkway Campus address automatically; anyone else can paste their own under **Different Campus address?**.
3. On the Campus website, run the saved **StudBud Import** bookmark (or, on desktop, copy the console script and paste it into the browser console). On Safari, choose the bookmark from Bookmarks. On Chrome for Android, type its name in the address bar and tap the saved bookmark suggestion, not a search result. Use a normal browser tab, not the Campus app or an in-app browser. It reads your data in your own signed-in session (StudBud never sees your password) and sends it back to StudBud when tab communication is available. Otherwise, tap **Copy data** in the helper and **Paste data from Campus** in StudBud. If clipboard access is blocked, choose **Show data**, select/copy it manually, and paste into StudBud's data box; downloading the .json and choosing that file also works.
4. Review the preview. Each class is matched to the closest PCHS catalog course (course number first, then name); change any match, keep the Campus name, or skip it. Then choose what to import.

The app tour introduces this importer at step 3, before dashboard explanations and manual class setup, and the About page lists it. Mobile setup instructions open automatically on touch/narrow screens.

Importing again updates the same classes and assignments instead of duplicating them, and keeps your study estimates and priorities. Imported class times, school days off, and day notes appear on the dashboard schedule and calendar. Remove them from the Schedule page at any time; your class periods stay saved.

## Optional email verification and sign-in

Creating an account requires only a username, password and school. **No email address or email verification is required**, and account creation signs you in immediately. Email is entirely opt-in from **Settings > Profile & Data > Optional email verification**. There is no new authenticator-app enrollment or QR setup.

If you choose to link an email, Supabase verifies that you own it before enabling email sign-in. The **same account ID** retains classes, decks, purchases, permissions and progress. After linking, use the verified email with your existing password in the main sign-in form, or expand **Sign in with an email code instead** and enter the inbox code inside StudBud. ## Accounts and cloud sync

New assignments default their due date to today in your local time zone. You can type in the date field or use its single built-in calendar icon. To connect the deployed static site to a Supabase project:

1. Create a Supabase project and run [`supabase-setup.sql`](./supabase-setup.sql) in the Supabase SQL editor as the default `postgres` role. If an older script failed with `42501: must be owner of table users`, rerun the full updated script; do not change ownership of the auth table. Existing case-insensitive duplicate usernames must be resolved before the claims backfill can succeed; the script does not silently discard duplicates.
   **End of beta:** on the existing project, also run [`beta-reset.sql`](./beta-reset.sql) once, after the setup script. It resets every account's study time, streaks, XP and level, coins (back to 100), cosmetics and daily rewards. Accounts, courses, grades, flashcard decks, friends, classes and game wins are kept. Rerunning it does nothing. The app also drops study sessions from before 2026-10-15 on every device.
2. Enable password authentication with an 8-character minimum. Keep TOTP verification enabled while any existing account still has an enrolled authenticator.
3. Copy the project URL and **publishable/anon key** into `url` and `anonKey` in [`supabase-config.js`](./supabase-config.js). These are public browser credentials; never put a service-role key in this file.
4. In Supabase **Authentication → Sign In / Providers → Email**, enable the Email provider and turn **off Confirm email**. This allows immediate username/password signup using the internal non-deliverable `@accounts.studbud.invalid` identifier; no verification email is sent at account creation. Allow new signups if account creation is desired. Configure custom SMTP for optional email verification/sign-in delivery to users, not just project-team addresses, and set the production Site URL.
5. In **Authentication → Email Templates**, include `<p>Your StudBud code is {{ .Token }}</p>` in **Magic Link / OTP** and **Change email address**. Keep the confirmation link in the change-email template as well so users can confirm their old address when required. The email form accepts 6–10 digit codes. The app enforces a 60-second resend interval; configure server-side rate limits and an appropriate OTP expiration too. Turning off signup confirmation does **not** bypass verification when an existing permanent account changes its email.
6. Rerun the full updated setup SQL **before linking existing admin emails**. It records the existing `overit` and `andy` admin account UUIDs once, so changing an email does not remove their access or grant it to a later account with the old address. For a new project, explicitly insert the intended admin's UUID into `public.studbud_admin_accounts` using the SQL editor; clients cannot edit that table.

Username-only accounts use non-deliverable `@accounts.studbud.invalid` addresses. Email-linked accounts can sign in with their inbox code without remembering a password. Accounts without a linked email have no email-based password recovery. Do not put personal data in the public username.

The first account used on a device adopts any existing local StudBud data into its cloud account. Signed-in changes sync automatically; sign in on another device to load the same account data. Manual backup export/import has been removed; normal sync and Infinite Campus imports remain available.

Cloud sync merges independent edits against the last saved baseline and retries concurrent server writes instead of replacing another device's changes. Pending local edits survive reload. **Question Chess** is a two-player real-time checkmate variant. Each correct question earns exactly **one move**, with at most one ready move held at once. There are **no regular alternating turns, energy settings, match timer or active-match expiry**: either player may move when ready, except that **only the checked player can move until escaping check**. Kings cannot be captured, and moving into check is illegal. Checkmate wins; stalemate or only kings remaining draws. Captured material starts at zero and uses standard values: pawn **1**, knight/bishop **3**, rook **5**, queen **9**. Winning is ranked separately from material points; draws do not select a material winner. Castling requires unmoved king/rook, clear squares and a safe king path. En passant is available immediately after the opponent's double pawn move, expiring on the next board move. Automatic queen promotion applies. The green-and-cream board uses one original, matching vector piece set for both colors and mode previews, with off-white and charcoal fills, dark outlines and subtle shading. Coordinates, last-move and check highlights, legal-move dots and capture rings clarify play. Piece movement animates unless reduced motion is enabled. A textured green gradient fills the game area, with responsive desktop, portrait and landscape layouts below the HUD. Keyboard/tap selection and mouse/touch drag moves are supported. Canceling an invalid drag preserves its credit. Solo practice has one simulated opponent.

Students can dismiss a message for the current page session; a new update makes it visible again.

Run the updated [`supabase-setup.sql`](./supabase-setup.sql) to enable full-deck inspection and announcements. These operations are administrator-checked on the server; the browser cannot grant access.

Only the latest active announcement appears in the banner until you dismiss it. Dismissal is saved to your account in Supabase and mirrored on the device after the save succeeds, so the same publication stays dismissed after reload, sign-in, or switching devices. Only publishing a new announcement creates a new banner; unpublishing does not reset dismissals or erase history. **Announcement history at the bottom of About** lists every archived publication chronologically newest first, including the current, dismissed and unpublished announcements. Each entry shows the publication date, time (in your local timezone), and publisher username; the newest entry is marked **Latest announcement**. The upgrade preserves the date/time and publisher of the currently stored announcement where available. An unavailable publisher is labeled **Site administrator**, not guessed. Saving a dismissal must succeed before the banner closes; any failure is shown so you can retry.

The publisher is captured from the authenticated administrator's account, not a hardcoded author list, so newly authorized admins are supported too. New announcements require a recorded username.

Rerun the full updated [`supabase-setup.sql`](./supabase-setup.sql) to install the announcement archive, author metadata and account dismissal functions. Apply the latest `supabase-setup.sql` to enable account-backed multiplayer profiles, purchases, random drops, personal-best syncing, and hosted rooms.
